## 1. One-paragraph summary

Uppteam is the brand that **International Service Partners, LLC (ISP)**, a Florida company, adopted on **1 Oct 2024**. ISP sells India-based architectural, MEP, structural, civil and BIM drafting/design support (plus virtual admin and "Second Eye" QA) to small and mid-size U.S. AEC firms. Production runs through **ISP Global Consulting Pvt Ltd** (Surat), with offices in Surat, Ahmedabad, Nashik and Kolkata. It is a real, founder-owned, bootstrapped business of roughly 175–260 people and an estimated $5–10M revenue, with no lawsuits or complaints on record. Weak points: inconsistent self-reported numbers, thin third-party proof, undisclosed promotion of an affiliate, and a website security issue.

## 6. Services and business model

**Services**
- **AOP (Architectural Offshore Production)** – flagship. Full CD sets at 30/60/100% in client standards, reviewed by a U.S. architect. Revit primary; delivery via Autodesk Construction Cloud.
- **Architectural design support** – SD/DD/CD, life-safety and ADA plans, renders and walkthroughs, as-builts.
- **MEP design support** – HVAC, electrical (NEC, photometrics, SKM), plumbing, fire protection, energy compliance (ASHRAE 90.1, COMcheck, Title 24).
- **Structural support** – Revit LOD 300–400, steel shop drawings, ETABS/SAP2000 seismic, permit sets under client's EOR.
- **Civil & survey support** – grading, drainage, utilities, SWPPP, land development, GIS (Civil 3D, ArcGIS).
- **BIM** – Scan-to-BIM, clash detection (Navisworks), Revit families, 4D/5D, CAD conversion.
- **Virtual admin** – dedicated back-office staff for contractors and architects (submittals, RFIs, document control). Monthly.
- **Second Eye (third-party QC)** – independent clash/model audits and CD quality checks; marketed as "guaranteed error-free".

**Commercial terms (published)**
- AOP: "fees are 35% of your typical architectural design budget" (fixed-fee style; hours saved = margin).
- Clutch: under $25/hr, $5,000 minimum project.
- Claims 40–60% savings vs in-house; quote in 24 hrs; 7–10 working-day turnaround; BIM ~2 weeks.
- No public rate card, trial or pilot.

**Delivery model**
- All production in India; 96% of AmbitionBox reviewers report day shift (so "24/7" = overnight turnaround).
- **UppAcademy:** 3 months free training → 3 months paid internship → employment.
- **Authorized Partner program:** U.S. architects (5+ yrs) front clients locally; Uppteam produces drawings.
- Liability: does not sign or seal; client's AOR/EOR stays responsible.

**Software claimed:** Revit (74 pages), AutoCAD, Bluebeam, Navisworks, BIM 360/ACC, Civil 3D, Tekla, ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD, SKM, AGi32, Trane TRACE, Carrier HAP, eQUEST, HydraCAD, Enscape, Lumion, Dynamo. Autodesk account confirmed via DNS verification record.

## 12. Market and competitors

**Market size (adjacent figures):** global drafting services ~$6B (2024, 5–7% CAGR); BIM modeling services ~$6.8B (2024, ~9.5% CAGR); total BIM ~$8–10B (~13% CAGR); North America BIM ~$3.05B (2025). **[Est.]** U.S. offshore drafting/BIM/design-support spend ~$1.2–1.9B/yr.

**Tailwinds:** structural engineer shortage (~18,000/yr shortfall, ACEC; ~half of firms turn down work); small architecture firms understaffed (53% of $250K–$1M billers in 2024); 75% of U.S. architecture firms have <10 staff; wide cost gap; BIM mandates; record 805 A/E M&A deals in 2025.

**Headwinds:** AIA billings index below 50 all of 2026; understaffed share down to 24%; AI drafting automation (Autodesk neural CAD claims 80–90% of routine design; Swapp, Augmenta, Motif, Hypar, TestFit); nearshore LatAm (~$3,900/mo, U.S. hours); India wage inflation ~9–10%/yr; responsible-control seal rules; CMMC/ITAR limits.

## 13. SWOT

- **Strengths:** PE founder + AIA Managing Principal; broadest bundle for small architecture firms; low entry ($5K min); UppAcademy pipeline; 15+ years, clean records.
- **Weaknesses:** thin/inconsistent proof; no ISO 27001/SOC 2; brand split (ISP/Uppteam); India-only delivery, no U.S.-hours overlap; junior-heavy teams.
- **Opportunities:** lead with engineering support; productize Second Eye (incl. QA of AI-generated documents); publish pod pricing; certify; serve PE-backed A/E roll-ups.
- **Threats:** AI automation of routine CD/BIM hours; soft U.S. architecture demand; nearshore LatAm; large Indian players moving downmarket; scrutiny of affiliate rankings and "EOR/stamped" wording.
