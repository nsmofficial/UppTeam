1. Executive Summary & Strategic Business Context

In commercial Architectural Offshore Production (AOP), manual Quality Control represents 20% to 35% of total engineering hours. This document outlines the 30-day rollout plan, 90-day scaling model, compute cost budget, and financial ROI framework for Uppteam.

Key Strategic Metrics:
• 30-Day Rollout: 4 weekly sprints from shadow-baselining to executive sign-off.
• QC Hours Saved: 40%–50% reduction in review cycle duration.
• Lean Infrastructure: $175/month cloud cost ($3.50/set) for 50 commercial sets/month.
• Internal Margin Gain: $71,280/year at target scale (50 sets/month).
• Commercial Revenue: $108,000/year new recurring revenue via external 'Second Eye' service.

2. 30-Day Phased Implementation Roadmap

Sprint Focus & Activities Key Deliverables Exit Criteria
Week 1 Shadow QC pods across 3 projects; baseline hours; finalize Zero Data Retention accord. Baseline QC metrics sheet; Security accord; CAD template index. Hours signed off; Security approved.
Week 2 Calibrate title blocks for top 5 client CAD standards; benchmark 10 historical sets. Custom CAD rules; historical eval report; precision/recall benchmark. Recall ≥ 85% on historical data; 0 parser crashes.
Week 3 Deploy review UI & folder watchers; shadow-pilot on 2 live projects alongside QC engineers. Interactive review portal; Bluebeam & Excel export pipelines; shadow log. Zero deadline impact; ≥ 80% reviewer acceptance.
Week 4 Analyze rejection rationales; tune confidence thresholds; conduct team training workshop. Calibrated pipeline; Engineer SOP; Executive sign-off report. Formal Go/No-Go sign-off; ≥ 35% QC time savings.

3. 90-Day Scaling & Commercialization Roadmap

  • Phase 2 (Days 31–60): Enterprise Rollout: Expand platform to 100% of internal AOP drafting teams. Direct integration with Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC) Issues API and automated BCF issue sync.
  • Phase 3 (Days 61–90): Commercial Launch: Productize as an external "Second Eye" AI QA/QC Service for U.S. and Canadian architectural firms at $450/set (24-hour turnaround, 80%+ gross margin).
